SMALL WONDERS / A POCKET FIELD GUIDE

Look a little closer.

Three things to notice on your next walk. Pick a note, take your time, and leave room for a small discovery.

01   The shape of a fern

SHADED PATHS / AFTER THE RAIN

A fern repeats its shape at a smaller scale. Follow one frond from its stem to its tip and look for the pattern inside the pattern.

New fronds begin as tightly curled fiddleheads, opening slowly toward the light.

Try this: sketch the outline with one continuous line.
02   A sky in a puddle

QUIET STREETS / BETWEEN SHOWERS

A shallow puddle can hold an entire sky. Move a little to one side and the reflection changes: branches, clouds, a passing bird.

Wait for a raindrop. Watch its circles cross the surface and disappear.

Try this: find three colors in a reflection.
03   The colors of lichen

OLD STONE / ANY SEASON

Look at a weathered wall. Pale green, soft gray and bright yellow patches make a landscape of their own.

Lichens grow slowly. Leave them where they are, and enjoy the details from a little closer.

Try this: give each shade a name of your own.
