A note in the margin

This box floats on the right. The article's lines use the space beside it, then return to the full width below.

E

Text has a shape. A paragraph can make room for an illustration, pass a note, and carry on below it. Floats ask the layout engine to find that space one line at a time.

This example combines left and right floats, their margins, and a separate formatting context around the article. The green initial and the margin note keep their own dimensions while the words adapt to the remaining width.

Try a narrower window. The available line width changes as the floats begin and end. The controls below use clearance to stay beneath the article's floating boxes.
